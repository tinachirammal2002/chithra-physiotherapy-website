SPORTS PHYSIOTHERAPY

Sports Injury Clinic

We are specialists in sports injuries. We are consultant to Sports Physiotherapy for Badminton and roller skating clubs. Our team have worked at the highest levels of competition across a variety of sports and our ability to understand what it is that goes into being a successful sports person means you will be in the right hands.

OUR APPROACH

Assessment, Treatment & Prevention

Our assessment and diagnostic skills are first class, and our treatment will ensure you are back at the top of your game in the peak of fitness.

We always ensure we address the cause and prioritise prevention strategies so that you can return to your sport with confidence and reduce the risk of future injuries.

Detailed Assessment

Precise assessment and diagnostic skills to understand the underlying cause of your injury.

Performance Focused

Treatment designed to help athletes return safely to their sport and peak performance.

Injury Prevention

Prevention strategies are prioritised to reduce the chance of recurring injuries.

Safe Recovery

A structured approach to recovery, fitness and a confident return to activity.

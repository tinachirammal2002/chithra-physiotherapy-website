OUR SPECIALISED CARE

Conditions We Assess & Treat

Our physiotherapists assess and treat infants and children with a wide range of paediatric conditions.

01

Cerebral Palsy

From mild hemiplegia to severe quadriplegia.

02

Developmental Delay

Developmental delay due to hypotonia, with or without a diagnosis, including gross motor or global delay.

03

Genetic Conditions

Syndromes and other genetic conditions affecting children's development and movement.

04

Spina Bifida

Spina bifida and neural tube defects.

05

Muscular Conditions

Muscular dystrophy and spinal muscular atrophy.

06

Brachial Plexus Lesions

Physiotherapy support for children with brachial plexus lesions.

07

Juvenile Chronic Arthritis

Physiotherapy management for juvenile chronic arthritis (JCA).

08

Visual Handicaps

Supporting movement, mobility and independence in children with visual handicaps.

09

Premature Babies

Physiotherapy care for premature babies with dystonia.

10

Postural Problems

Torticollis, scoliosis, talipes, metatarsus adductus and idiopathic toe walking.

11

Respiratory Problems

Conditions such as cystic fibrosis and asthma.

12

Osteogenesis Imperfecta

Physiotherapy support for children with osteogenesis imperfecta.

13

Minimal Cerebral Dysfunction

Individualised physiotherapy support to improve movement and functional abilities.

ACTIVE CHILDHOOD

Encouraging Activity & Independence

We encourage children to partake in a wide range of activities at school and in the community to maintain physical fitness and provide opportunities for socialisation with their peers.

We also prescribe and monitor the use of aids such as orthotics, walking aids and wheelchairs to help children maintain independence and participate more confidently in their daily activities.

Physical Fitness

Encouraging regular activity to support strength, mobility and overall fitness.

Social Participation

Supporting children to participate in school and community activities.

Mobility & Independence

Appropriate aids can help children maintain independence in daily life.

Orthotic Support

Prescribing and monitoring orthotics and other mobility aids when required.

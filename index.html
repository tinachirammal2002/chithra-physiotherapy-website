Dr. Vinod Raj's Surgery free solution to Disc problems

EXPERT CARE Dr. Vinod Raj

With over 28 years of dedicated experience, Dr. Vinod Raj is a pioneer in the field of physiotherapy, renowned for his expertise in treating disc bulge and spine-related conditions without surgery. Since establishing his clinic in Pathanamthitta in 1998, he has successfully treated more than 10,000 disc bulge patients from across India and abroad, helping them regain mobility and live pain-free lives without surgery.

Dr. Vinod Raj's approach combines scientific physiotherapy methods, advanced rehabilitation techniques, and personalized care. His proven track record has earned him the trust of patients worldwide who seek his guidance for effective, non-invasive solutions to spine and musculoskeletal problems.

In addition to his clinical practice, he shares his knowledge through his popular YouTube channel Chitra Physiotherapy, which has over 200,000 subscribers and has been recognized with YouTube's Silver Play Button Award.

Today, Dr. Vinod Raj continues to lead with a mission: to provide patients safe, result-oriented, and surgery-free recovery options, making him one of the most respected physiotherapists in Kerala and beyond.

STROKE REHABILITATION

Comprehensive Stroke Rehabilitation

The whole rehabilitation program at Chitra Physiotherapy Clinic is predicated on two general components. The first includes preventive measures targeted at maintaining physical integrity and minimizing complications that will prevent or prolong functional return.

These measures should begin immediately post stroke and continue as long as necessary.

OUR REHABILITATION APPROACH

Two Important Components

Our rehabilitation program focuses on both preventing complications and promoting functional recovery.

01

Preventive Measures

The first component focuses on maintaining physical integrity and minimizing complications that may prevent or prolong functional return.

Initiated immediately post stroke
Continued as long as necessary
02

Restorative Treatment

The second component is restorative treatment aimed at promoting functional recovery and improving the patient's ability to participate actively in rehabilitation.

Begins when medically and neurologically stable
Encourages active participation
RESTORATIVE PHASE

Individualized Rehabilitation

Restorative treatment should begin as soon as the patient is medically and neurologically stable and has the cognitive and physical ability to participate actively in a rehabilitation program.

PHYSIOTHERAPY GOALS

Our Aims of Physical Therapy

Our physical therapy interventions are designed to support recovery and improve quality of life.

01

Promote Motor Recovery

Supporting improvement in movement and motor function during rehabilitation.

02

Optimize Sensory Functions

Helping optimize sensory functions as part of the overall rehabilitation process.

03

Enhance Functional Independence

Encouraging greater independence in functional activities and everyday life.

04

Prevent Secondary Complications

Helping reduce the risk of secondary complications through appropriate rehabilitation interventions.
